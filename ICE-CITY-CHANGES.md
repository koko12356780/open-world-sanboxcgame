# Ice City - v0.10

- A blue swirling portal and tall beacon on the northwest downtown tower roof.
- Fly the Roadster through the ring at about 68 m to enter a separate ice city.
- A 650 m city grid with frozen roads, a frozen lake and a 1.4 km glacier road.
- Icy buildings, snow caps, pine trees, crystal formations and falling snow.
- Winter lighting and a night aurora; N still switches day/night.
- Reduced tire grip on the frozen district; normal grip on returning.
- A return portal usable by any car, with HUD directions and radar markers.
- District-aware repair/reset, save/load and Discord area display.
- Existing cars, upgrades, workshop, flight and rear thrusters retained.

Ice City is generated on first entry. Original scenery and collision bodies
are disabled while visiting, then restored on return. Swept portal detection
catches fast crossings. Loading old saves defaults to the original city.

All scenery is original procedural artwork. This is a stylized starter game.
