# Street Explorer - v0.11

- E exits / re-enters your own parked car; WASD walks, Shift runs, Space jumps.
- First-person mouse look, solid building collisions, footsteps, pause support.
- R recovers the player to the repaired car. Flying/fast cars cannot be exited.
- Walking works in the city and Ice City. Nearby scenery follows the pedestrian.
- Garage and district changes return the player safely to the driving camera.
- Larger business names, layered awnings, door handles and OPEN signs.
- Original illustrated cafe, market and service-bay window displays.
- Walking controls are available in the pause menu and on-screen hints.
- Current car/coins/upgrades are retained. Loading resumes in the parked car.

Companion launcher v1.5 adds a configurable permanent HTTPS update feed,
startup checks, streamed ZIP downloads, checksums, cancellation and rollback.
The creator can paste a GitHub repository link and choose Prepare Release Files.
No hosted feed is included until a repository/website is configured.

All storefronts are scenery; shops cannot yet be entered. Visuals are original
procedural meshes, shaders, textures and vector window illustrations.
